Disclaimer & Safety Notice

Last updated: August 7, 2026

What ShapeGap Is

ShapeGap provides general fitness information and logging features. It is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for the judgment of a physician or other qualified professional.

Before You Start

  • If you have a medical condition, consult a physician before using the App.
  • The same applies if you are pregnant or under treatment.
  • If you have not exercised for a long time, start with light loads.
  • The App may not be used by anyone under 16.

Stop Training If You Experience

  • Chest pain
  • Severe dizziness
  • Persistent shortness of breath
  • Sudden joint pain
  • Fever or other signs of illness
  • Sudden loss of strength or sharp pain

If any of these occur, stop exercising immediately. Seek medical care if needed.

About the Numbers

  • Calories and macros (protein, fat, carbohydrates) are estimates based on food databases. Actual values may differ.
  • The App does not measure body-fat percentage and never claims a definitive body-fat figure from a photo.

About AI

  • Plan descriptions and explanations are generated by AI. Their accuracy is not guaranteed.
  • Weight and rep targets and macro totals are calculated by fixed rules, not by AI.
  • Plans take effect only after you review and approve them.
