Canceling Your Subscription

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name (account).
  3. Tap Subscriptions, choose ShapeGap, and cancel.

If you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, you will not be charged again. After canceling, you keep access to paid features until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

Refunds are handled under Apple's policies. Please request a refund through Apple's refund page.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and data from the settings screen in the App. Deletion also removes your photos, logs, and derived files from our servers.

Reporting Rights Infringement

If you believe your copyright, portrait rights, or other rights are being infringed in connection with the App, contact us at the email address above. We will confirm the details needed to identify the content and respond in accordance with applicable law.
