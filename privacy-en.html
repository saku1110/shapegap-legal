ShapeGap Privacy Policy

Effective date / last updated: August 7, 2026

Toshiki Sakuraya (the "Operator") handles personal information in the application "ShapeGap" (the "App") as described below.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information (email address, authentication ID)
  • Birth year (for age verification; we collect the year only, not your full date of birth)
  • Body data (height, weight, body-composition values you enter)
  • Workout logs and food logs
  • Body photos (current) and goal photos (optional)
  • Pain and condition entries
  • Gym equipment information
  • Calendar-integration data (if you enable it; we store only time-slot mappings, never event contents)
  • Device information and usage logs
  • Purchase history

3. How Photos Are Handled

  • Photos are stored in private storage and served only through short-lived signed URLs. No other user can view them.
  • Photos are sent to an external AI service when the current-vs-goal comparison feature runs, as described in the next section.
  • When you delete your account, your photos and derived files such as thumbnails are deleted as well.
  • We never use your photos for advertising, promotion, or tracking.

4. Data Sent to AI Services

  • We send the following to external AI (large language model) providers: (a) your current body photo and goal photo, when running the comparison feature; (b) summaries of your goals, body data, and logs, when generating plan descriptions.
  • We use contractual terms (data processing agreements) that prohibit providers from using your data to train their models.
  • AI is used only to generate plan text and to compare photos. Numeric calculations such as weights, reps, calories, and macros are not performed by AI.

5. Pain and Condition Entries

Pain and condition entries are collected solely for safety features, such as adjusting training load and advising you to stop. They are not used for medical purposes and are not shared with medical institutions or any other third party.

7. International Transfers

Because of the processing above, your data is processed on servers in the United States. The Operator takes appropriate protective measures, including contracts with processors, as required by applicable law.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete your account at any time from the settings screen in the App.
  • When you delete your account, photos, logs, and derived files on our servers are deleted without undue delay. Complete removal from backups may take up to about 30 days.

9. Your Rights

You may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of your personal data. Contact us at the email address below. We will verify your identity and respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. Tracking and Advertising

  • The App shows no behavioral advertising and includes no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We never use your health-related data or photos for advertising or tracking.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

11. Children

The App may not be used by anyone under 16 years of age.

12. Data Breaches

If a breach of personal data occurs, we will report to the competent authority and notify affected users in accordance with applicable law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as laws or App features change. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
